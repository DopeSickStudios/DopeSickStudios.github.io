How to play

You have inherited Gran's pet shop. Care for the animals, open the shop, and find each one a good home.

  • Walk Tap a spot, or steer with the stick at the bottom left.
  • Use Tap an animal, a shelf or a person, or press the round button at the bottom right.
  • Menus Your journal, decorating and the menu are at the top right.
  • Saving Sleeping in your bed saves the day, on this device. Leave any time: Continue brings you back.

Full screen: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Do it before you start a game: the new icon keeps its own saves.

Please rotate your device

Marigold Lane is played in landscape.

If the picture does not turn, switch off the rotation lock.
